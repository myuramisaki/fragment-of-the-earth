FRAGMENTS OF EARTH

The year is 2087. The sky is no longer blue. It burns with a sickly violet glow.


Three months ago, they arrived. Massive silver spheres descended from the clouds — silent, impossible, and terrifying. Within hours, the cities fell. Not by bombs, but by something far worse.


"People of Earth. We are the Voryx. Your world is forfeit. But we are not without mercy. We offer you a game."


The message played on every screen, every speaker, everywhere.


"Twenty of your kind shall be chosen. They will be taken to the Nexus Realm. Scattered across this realm are five fragments of a Master Key. Find them all, assemble the Key, and the portal home opens. Fail and humanity is erased. Forever."


Millions volunteered. Only twenty were selected. You are one of them.

A blinding light engulfs you. The world dissolves.
Welcome to the Nexus.

THE TWENTY CHOSEN

You stand in a vast, circular chamber. Nineteen others stand with you — from every corner of Earth. Fear is in their eyes. But so is hope.

A hologram appears before you — the Voryx entity, tall and shimmering.

"The rules are simple. Each fragment lies within a different domain. Each domain holds a challenge. Complete it, claim your fragment. There is no death here — only failure. But remember: if all 20 fail your species ends. Good luck."

The floor beneath you begins to glow. Symbols light up in rings around your feet.

This room is different. It looks exactly like Earth — your Earth. Blue sky, green grass, a gentle breeze. But it is all a simulation.

The fifth fragment rests on a pedestal in the center. As you approach, the Voryx appear one last time.

"One final test. Weave energy from point to point. Draw the circuit — no crossing lines, no lifting until complete. The shape of your survival."

MISSION COMPLETE

The five fragments float before you, drawn together by an invisible force. They click into place, glowing with brilliant golden light. The Master Key is whole.

A massive portal opens — beyond it, Earth. Waiting. Beautiful. Damaged, but alive.

"You have proven yourselves worthy. The game is concluded. Go home, Children of Earth. Protect what you have earned."

You step through. The air is fresh. People cheer as you emerge from the rift. The sun rises — not violet, but golden. A new beginning.

YOU SAVED HUMANITY.

FAILURE

The fragments slip from your grasp. The Voryx appear — not angry, but disappointed.

"The time has run out. Your species had its chance. Farewell."

The portal closes. The Nexus dissolves into nothingness. Silence falls across the universe.

HUMANITY WAS NOT SAVED.
